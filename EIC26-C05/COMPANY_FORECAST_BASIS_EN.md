## Forecast assumptions

1. All five columns are forecasts: N=2027; N+1=2028; N+2=2029; N+3=2030; N+4=2031. The template Actual (if applicable) caption is not a claim of actual 2027 data.
2. Company management reports EUR300000 cash and no outstanding debt or short-term liabilities on3November2026. Carrying EUR300000 into1January2027 is an explicit forecast assumption, requiring the intervening burn bridge. The source opening cash and legacy debt/accrual are reconciled through opening forecast net assets, not a new cash contribution or debt forgiveness.
3. The old forecast legacy principal EUR250000 and opening accrued interest EUR10208.33 are removed. This removes EUR250000 financing repayment and EUR13125 operating interest settlement in2027, while removing only EUR2916.67 current-year interest expense. The opening equity adjustment identifies those balances and the opening-cash change explicitly. Future term-debt draws remain conditional forecasts.
4. Unconverted subscription instruments are presented within other forecast equity, without assigning a BSA-AIR conversion percentage or a future legal share allocation. The saved model balance is a bookkeeping projection; the current instrument ledger and next-round terms determine actual legal economics.
5. Saved company operating revenue and baseline source costs are retained. This conservative scenario adds the complete EIC qualification and commercial launch programme to baseline costs; no automatic absorption or operating saving is assumed. Reconcile baseline allocations before final management adoption to remove any proven duplicate, not by treating new work as free.
6. Cash gross margin adds back source production D&A; all D&A is deducted once in row24. Sales/admin and bad debt use the A14 source cost bridge. Other revenues/expenses is net finance expense less accrued EIC grant; a grant is negative in this subtractive official formula.
7. Grant cash follows the explicit 60/25/15 planning scenario; grant income accrues with planned eligible work. Deferred grant or accrued grant receivable reconciles income to cash. No CETP funding is used as EIC co-finance.
8. Capital assets follow phased ready-for-use dates and 36/60-month useful-life estimates. Post-action use and depreciation continue in the company forecast, without charging those later costs to the grant. Serial tooling is capitalised in construction before its M26 completion and depreciated thereafter over60months.
9. WP6 initial inventory and receivables are working-capital balances, not duplicate P&L expenditure. Existing company revenue includes the C05 subset; project sales are not added again.
10. Future gross equity targets EUR8m in2027,25m in2028,70m in2030 are nominal EUR management plans. Saved absolute issuance fees remain; cashflow row38 shows proceeds net of these fees. No FX, tax engine or company operating engine is rerun.
11. Latest registered capital EUR65533 from13September2026 Kbis is held in the planning presentation. Future contributed finance is allocated to other equity pending instrument terms and the current fully diluted schedule. The forecast does not assert future legal nominal capital.
12. Current debt split uses the saved next12-month term-principal schedule; current liquidity and legal instrument facts are requested once in the consolidated completion list. No actual cash balance is inferred from source model opening cash.
