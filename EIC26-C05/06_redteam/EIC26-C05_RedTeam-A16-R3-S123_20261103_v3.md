### Executive summary (not in R3; replaces the R1 summary)

> Extreme heat now strikes Europe's cities when their grids and water supplies are already stretched. Data centres cannot shut down: they must keep cooling exactly when compression chillers lose efficiency and grid headroom disappears. AirTransformer removes that conflict by cooling with surplus heat instead of a compressor.
>
> A staged metal-organic framework (MOF) heat-and-mass exchanger dries the incoming air and is regenerated with 40–70 °C heat: 50–70 °C return water from liquid-cooled racks where liquid water is recovered, 40–50 °C industrial waste heat where it is not. An indirect evaporative stage then cools the dried air. No compression takes place in the cooling process, so electricity drives only fans and pumps. Savery's complete machine is at TRL6. Tested in 2025 under EN conditions with 60 °C waste heat, it delivered sensible cooling at a COP of 20; in installations, COP ranges from 8 to 20, against 3 to 4 for compression cooling in a heatwave. Each MW of cooling therefore frees 208–283 kW of grid capacity in the hottest hours, and external make-up water falls by 40–60%.
>
> The product is built from 20 kW modules, assembled into 600 kW containerised units. Savery owns its technology through pending applications: the 2025 heat-and-mass-exchanger family (FR2508386 and its PCT extension), a 2026 European application for a sorption heat pump whose low-pressure-ratio vapour compressor reaches heating COP 6–10 (EP26301484.7), and the 2019 system family. Its scientific board includes Norbert Stock and Aline Auroux. An awarded CETP project (2026–2029) is taking the system to TRL7 at an HPE site.
>
> The EIC action turns the machine into an industrial product: a manufacturable design, qualified CAU contactor batches, reliability and conformity evidence, and TRL8 qualification by March 2029. Data-centre cooling is a USD 20.5 bn market; Savery's plan reaches €180.9 m revenue in 2032, about 1% of it. We request a €2,485,583 grant (70% of €3,550,833 eligible cost) and €2 m EIC investment within an €8 m Seed round closing in November 2027.
