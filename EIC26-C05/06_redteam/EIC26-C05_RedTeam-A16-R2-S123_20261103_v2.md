- **Performance:** COP 20 is measured. Installed COP is 8 to 20, against 3 to 4 for compression cooling in a heatwave.
- **Novelty:** there is no compressor in the cooling cycle, which is the root of that advantage.
- **Product:** a 20 kW module, combined into 600 kW containers.
- **IP:** two new Savery filings plus the 2019 family.
- **Market:** a €15 bn data-centre cooling SAM, against which the 2032 plan is a credible 1.2%.
- **Customer economics:** the primary customer, a liquid-cooled data centre, pays back in 25–36 months at €0.20–0.25/kWh with no further assumptions.

### A. Executive summary (replace in full; about 330 words)

> Extreme heat now strikes Europe's cities when their grids and water supplies are already stretched. Data centres cannot shut down: they must keep cooling exactly when compression chillers lose efficiency and grid headroom disappears. AirTransformer removes that conflict by cooling with surplus heat instead of a compressor.
>
> A staged metal-organic framework (MOF) heat-and-mass exchanger dries the incoming air and is regenerated with 40–70 °C heat: 50–70 °C return water from liquid-cooled racks where liquid water is recovered, and 40–50 °C industrial waste heat, such as air-compressor cooling circuits, where it is not. An indirect evaporative stage then cools the dried air. No compression takes place in the cooling process, so electricity drives only fans and pumps. Savery's complete machine is at TRL6. Tested in 2025 under EN conditions with 60 °C waste heat, it delivered sensible cooling at a COP of 20. Installed COP ranges from 8 to 20 with site pumping, against 3 to 4 for compression cooling in a heatwave. Each MW of cooling therefore frees 208–283 kW of grid capacity in the hottest hours, and external make-up water falls by 40–60%.
>
> The product is a 20 kW module, combined into 600 kW containerised units. Savery owns its technology: the 2025 heat-and-mass-exchanger family (FR2508386 and its PCT extension), a 2026 European application for a sorption heat pump that reaches heating COP 6–10 with a low-pressure-ratio vapour compressor (EP26301484.7), and the 2019 system family. Its scientific board includes Norbert Stock and Aline Auroux. An awarded CETP project (2026–2029) is taking the system to TRL7 at an HPE site.
>
> The EIC action turns the machine into an industrial product: a manufacturable design, qualified CAU contactor batches, reliability and conformity evidence, and TRL8 qualification by March 2029. Data-centre cooling is a €15 bn annual market; Savery's plan reaches €180.9 m revenue in 2032, 1.2% of it. We request a €2,485,583 grant (70% of €3,550,833 eligible cost) and €2 m EIC investment within an €8 m Seed round closing in November 2027.

### B. §1.1, first subsection (S1 l.5–13): replace the heading and the first two paragraphs; keep paragraph 3 ("three engineering levels"); add two paragraphs after it

> ### Cooling without compression
>
> AirTransformer cools without a compressor. A staged MOF heat-and-mass exchanger removes moisture from the process air and is regenerated with surplus heat at 40–70 °C. An indirect evaporative stage then removes sensible heat from the dried air. Because no vapour is compressed in the cooling process, electricity drives only fans and pumps.
>
> Savery's complete machine is at TRL6. Tested in 2025 under EN conditions with a 60 °C waste-heat supply, it delivered sensible cooling at a COP of 20, measured as delivered sensible cooling divided by electricity consumed (Annex «test report»). The EN set-up connects the heat loops at static pressure, so circulation pumps lie outside the test boundary; installed COP ranges from 8 to 20 with site pumping and adjacent components. Vapour-compression cooling operates at 3 to 4 in heatwave conditions. Data centres facing heatwaves on constrained grids are the first customers.
>
> *(keep: "The innovation connects three engineering levels…")*
>
> The heat source sets the configuration. Liquid-cooled racks return water at 50–70 °C, enough to regenerate the sorbent and recover liquid water from the air. Without water recovery, 40–50 °C is sufficient, so any industrial waste heat, such as air-compressor cooling circuits, can drive the machine. The product is built from 20 kW modules, assembled into 600 kW containerised units.
>
> The same sorption core extends to heating. Savery's 2026 machine adds a water-vapour compressor operating at a low pressure ratio and reaches a heating COP of 6 to 10. Two Savery application families protect these developments (Section 1.3).
