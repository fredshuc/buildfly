## Verdict

R4 is the first version that can win GO. It adopts the applicant facts:
- the measured sensible-cooling COP of 20 and the installed range of 8–20;
- the 20 kW module and 600 kW container;
- the two heat-source modes;
- heating COP 6–10;
- the €15 bn market estimate;
- the Seed in November 2027.

It also meets the template: headings, six-column team table, governance table, compliance paragraph and CVs. Customer economics now hold inside the 24–36-month objective, and annual ROI of 35–48% is shown.

**Five items must be fixed before submission. Each is under an hour.**

1. **Figure 1 integrity (high risk).**
   - The "AI" container shows third-party logos. In a proposal, logos imply partnerships.
   - The SEM tile is labelled 10⁻⁵ m, but its scale bar reads 500 µm (5 × 10⁻⁴ m). Its data bar also contains a literal "label" field. An expert will check a micrograph.
   - The equipment render carries a flame icon, which reads as combustion in a waste-heat proposal.
   - Fix: remove the logos (or keep only partners who have agreed in writing), use a real Savery micrograph with a consistent scale (or drop the data bar), and replace the flame render with a **photo of the actual TRL6 machine**. That photo is also the strongest TRL evidence the template asks for ("Please provide evidence of TRL 5 (or higher) validation"), and R4 has none.
2. **Executive summary and challenge-fit statement are missing.** Both are required by the template. Text is below.
3. **The COP 20 test cannot be "referred to in an annex".** The template's annex list is closed (pitch, video, lump-sum budget, implementation plan, financial plan, FTO, LOIs), and experts ignore hyperlinks. Add one reference line (report title, date, laboratory) so the result is traceable at interview. If one rating point (inlet air state, capacity) fits in the table, add it.
4. **Three sentences in Section 3 undermine the team and the financing** (S3 l.17, l.83, l.110). Text below.
5. **Page budget.** The R4 bodies total about 9,400 words plus about 640 words of references, 20 tables and a figure. With the summary and challenge statement added, the merged Part B is at risk of exceeding 20 pages (R1 was 19 pages at 11,055 words with far fewer tables). The cut list below removes about 1,000 words without losing content.

Residual risks that text cannot remove:
- a three-person core team with 82.6% of action effort still to be recruited;
- no named customer or investor letters of intent;
- no BSA-AIR traction figure.

The first is answered as well as it can be. The other two need one input each (end of this file).

**Executive summary (about 300 words; replaces the R1 summary).**

> Extreme heat now hits Europe's cities when grids and water supplies are already stretched. Data centres cannot pause: they must keep cooling when compression chillers lose efficiency and grid headroom disappears. AirTransformer cools with surplus heat instead of a compressor.
>
> A staged metal-organic framework (MOF) heat-and-mass exchanger dries the incoming air and is regenerated with waste heat: 50–70 °C from liquid-cooled racks, with liquid-water recovery, or 40–50 °C from industrial sources such as air-compressor circuits. An indirect evaporative stage then cools the dried air. The cooling process uses no vapour compression, so fans and pumps are the main electrical loads. Savery's complete machine is at TRL6: in its 2025 EN test it delivered sensible cooling at an electrical COP of 20 with 60 °C waste heat. Installed, COP ranges from 8 to 20, against 3 for compression cooling in a heatwave, so each MW of cooling releases 208–283 kW of grid capacity. A liquid-cooled data centre recovers its investment in 25–35 months at €0.25/kWh.
>
> The product comprises 20 kW modules and 600 kW containers. Savery's pending applications protect the staged exchanger (FR2508386 and its PCT extension, 2025) and a sorption heat pump reaching heating COP 6–10 (EP26301484.7, 2026). Norbert Stock and Aline Auroux sit on its scientific board. An awarded CETP project (2026–2029) is taking the system to TRL7 at an HPE site.
>
> The EIC action makes the machine an industrial product: qualified CAU contactor batches, reliability and conformity evidence, field operation and TRL8 by March 2029. Savery's plan reaches €180.9 m revenue in 2032 in a USD 20.5 bn data-centre cooling market. We request a €2,485,583 grant (70% of €3,550,833 eligible cost) and €2 m EIC investment within an €8 m Seed round closing in November 2027.
