## Abstract

AirTransformer provides climate-resilient cooling for urban critical facilities exposed to extreme heat, electricity constraints and water stress. Savery Tech has a complete TRL6 machine integrating sorption air treatment, thermal management and indirect evaporative cooling. Its CAU-series material route uses low-temperature regeneration heat. Product qualification targets 60–70% lower electricity demand and 40–60% lower external make-up water at equivalent cooling service, within declared source, sink and climate conditions. Savery owns its 2019 system patent family. An awarded CETP grant supports its ongoing 2026–2029 programme towards TRL7, with HPE providing the site. The proposed EIC action adds repeatable manufacturing, batch qualification, reliability, conformity evidence and independent product acceptance towards TRL8. Its own qualification route avoids dependence on later CETP deliverables, and each cost is allocated once. The 24-month action plans April 2027 to March 2029, with €3,550,833.33 eligible cost and €2,485,583.33 grant requested. Savery also seeks €2 million EIC investment within an €8 million Seed target, alongside €6 million from other sources, for co-financing, manufacturing deployment, service capability and working capital.

## AI development and use

Yes. The action develops and uses model-based supervisory control and may retain a bounded reinforcement-learning optimisation layer for setpoints or cycle decisions. Independent physical limits, safety interlocks, sensor diagnostics, anomaly detection, operator override and deterministic fallback retain authority outside the learning policy. Development separates training, calibration and final acceptance data, covers relevant climate/load conditions and held-out disturbances, and tests actuator faults, sensor drift, confidence loss and safe recovery. Versioned validation and change control precede product release. The proposed use is industrial cooling control; a legal high-risk classification is not inferred solely from the presence of AI.
