EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
====================================================================

Extreme heat is now Europe's deadliest climate hazard: an estimated 61,672 people died of heat in summer 2022 (Ballester et al., Nature Medicine, 2023). Cities are hit hardest. The buildings that must stay cool during a heatwave, such as hospitals, care homes, campuses and public buildings, rely on vapour-compression air conditioning. To remove humidity, that equipment overcools the air and then reheats it. This drives peak electricity demand on the hottest days and rejects heat into streets that are already overheated.

AirTransformer separates the two jobs. A metal-organic framework (MOF) sorbent removes the moisture and is regenerated with low-grade heat, so only the sensible load needs electrically driven cooling. «FILL: one sentence on the core architecture, verbatim from the 2025 Part B.» The core unit has completed TRL 5. It was tested to ASHRAE 143 and EN 13141-7 on ISO 17025-calibrated instrumentation «FILL: test site, date, report ID». It delivers 16 to 20 °C supply air at 9 to 11 g/kg while using 60 to 70% less electricity and 40 to 60% less water than «FILL: reference system named in the 2025 test report».

For the customer, this means a 24 to 36-month payback at an installed cost of €1,100/kW, falling to €900/kW at volume. It also lowers peak grid load during heatwaves, which matters most for facilities that must keep operating. The first customers are critical urban facilities in regions of the EU Adaptation Mission. Data centres serve as early adopters because they have surplus heat that can regenerate the sorbent at no cost.

The project takes the validated core to TRL 8 «FILL: confirm target TRL and pilot sites». It adds three increments to that core: a second sorbent stage, a condensate-recovery branch and a supervisory control layer. Each one is gated on a measured gain over the core.

Savery Tech owns the system IP «FILL: application numbers». A European freedom-to-operate analysis covers BASF, NuMat, Framergy and AirJoule. Sorbent supply has a commercial fallback that does not depend on any single licence.

1.1 BREAKTHROUGH INNOVATION AND STATE OF THE ART
====================================================================

The problem is latent load. In hot, humid urban conditions, moisture removal can account for «FILL: latent share of total cooling load, cite source» of the cooling load. Incumbent equipment handles it in one of three ways. Each way has a structural cost:

Vapour compression with reheat. The coil must run below the dew point, so the system operates at low evaporator temperature and poor COP, and then spends energy to reheat the air. This is the dominant installed base and the reference for our savings figures.

Desiccant wheels (silica gel, zeolite). Moisture is removed thermally, but regeneration typically needs 80 to 140 °C. That usually means a burner or electric heater rather than recovered heat, and it adds a large post-cooling load.

MOF sorption units. This is the emerging class that AirTransformer belongs to. The closest competitor is AirJoule (US). Its public product documentation describes paired MOF-coated chambers regenerated under vacuum with low-grade heat: about 60 °C geothermal water in its Hubbard, Texas pilot. Its CORE unit is rated at up to 250 L/day of water with a target of 0.50 kWh/L when paired with a heat pump. After acquiring BitSink, AirJoule is targeting data centres. Its first European unit is due at the Net Zero Innovation Hub testbed in Fredericia, Denmark, in Q4 2026.

Supervisory control (one paragraph only, because the AI declaration triggers a robustness assessment). A model-predictive controller (MPC) runs the unit and enforces hard limits on supply-air temperature, humidity and regeneration temperature. Above it, a reinforcement-learning supervisor proposes only setpoints, such as switching times and regeneration temperature. Every proposal must pass the MPC constraint layer before it reaches the hardware. The supervisor is first trained offline on the validated unit model, then run in shadow mode at the pilots. It is enabled only once it beats MPC alone by «FILL: threshold» with no constraint violations. If it fails or behaves unexpectedly, the unit falls back to MPC alone, which is the configuration validated at TRL 5.

Increments are therefore additions to a validated core, not new research: a second sorbent stage, a condensate-recovery branch giving «FILL: L/day per unit at a stated design climate, OR delete this and keep water as a co-benefit», and supervisory control.
