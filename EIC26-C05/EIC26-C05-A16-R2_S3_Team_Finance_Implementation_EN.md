## 3.1 Team capability

### Ownership, continuity and organisational equality

CURMA SOLUTIONS holds Savery Tech's issued shares. The signed declaration of 7 April 2026 records 100% of issued capital and voting rights. Current investor financing uses unconverted BSA-AIR instruments; conversion valuation follows the next financing. A 10% option pool is reserved for key members. Issued holdings, reserved incentives and future conversion are kept distinct when preparing the fully diluted schedule. [S3-1]

Controlled drawings, software, test procedures and decision records support continuity. Qin prepares build/release cover; the reliability lead assigns test-operation cover; Ina maintains acceptance records. Documented architecture decisions support work when the founder is unavailable.

The organisation will pursue a target of women representing at least 40% of newly recruited employees by M24, March 2029. Ina implements the recruitment and progression measures; Frédéric is accountable for the target and quarterly review. The target is prospective. It covers technical and non-technical recruitment, with scientific-board participation and external suppliers reported separately from the employee denominator.

From M1, role descriptions and interview scoring use consistent skills criteria. Recruitment channels will reach women in materials, thermal engineering, controls, manufacturing and technical operations. Quarterly reviews examine applications, hiring, retention, training and progression by role family, including technician and non-management positions. Management will act on the stage where an avoidable imbalance occurs while retaining the same competence and safety requirements. Pay review, development opportunities, workable caring-leave arrangements and appropriately fitted workplace equipment support retention as the organisation grows.

### CETP research and EIC product implementation

CETP is an awarded, ongoing 2026–2029 programme targeting TRL7 with HPE providing the site. Its research models, heat-and-mass-transfer studies, cycle maps, digital-twin/RL research and agreed HPE integration form relevant background. EIC work uses those results to create distinct industrial outputs.

| Existing/CETP contribution | EIC product output | Evidence and cost separation |
|---|---|---|
| Research models, transport parameters and cycle maps | Calibration for the released machine, rating/verification methods and parameter records | Model/input version linked to product test data; identifiable product-validation time and tests |
| Digital-twin/RL research | Bounded supervisory implementation, comparison cases and regression/fallback verification | Product requirements, versioned code, held-out validation and fault/recovery reports |
| Research control concepts | Embedded interfaces, diagnostics, hardware integration and deployable software release | Hardware/software configuration, interface tests and release record |
| Material characterisation and research process knowledge | Industrial process window, batch quality and manufacturing transfer | EIC lot, assay, operator-time and release identifiers |
| CETP/HPE integrated demonstration | Incremental product-specific interfaces, acceptance, reliability and replication evidence | Defined extra test purpose, access/utilities and project-coded cost records |
| Existing equipment and funded capability | Attributable use of dedicated product-qualification assets and independent facilities | Ready-for-use dates, asset/use logs, test IDs and invoices allocated once |

The two 18-PM thermal-validation and supervisory-control roles retain 36 PM of product work. Their deliverables are calibration, verification, industrial control and regression evidence. The separate 18-PM embedded-software role delivers deployable interfaces and diagnostics. Similar model or control terminology in two projects does not by itself determine duplicate effort; allocation follows the actual task, output and employee time.

Before work is charged, the project-data resource and finance support reconcile those outputs against the funded CETP work plan. A duplicated task or cost is removed from one allocation, with the change recorded. Reusing a research result or an accessible dataset does not recharge its original creation cost. The same rule applies to assets, laboratory time, field services and invoices.

The EIC completion sequence uses the existing machine, dedicated unit and independent qualification capacity through March 2029. The planned independent climate and endurance route supplies conditions unavailable in the field window. HPE remains the actual CETP site; incremental EIC access, interfaces and operating responsibilities are secured for their own scope. A later CETP result is incorporated into a later version where appropriate, rather than silently moving the M24 evidence gate.

### Principal execution risks and responses

| Risk and delivery consequence | Response and accountable owner | Review point |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment delays affect the 213 PM planned-role allocation | Frédéric and Qin prioritise the M1 posts, match selection to first deliverables, assign deputies and rephase dependencies; scoped professional services support the tasks they actually cover | Before M1 commitments, then monthly and before each gate |
| CAU processing changes reduce output, consistency or life | Process and reliability leads use batch traceability, after-shaping tests, ageing/contamination checks and quarantined nonconforming lots | Batch releases and M12/M18 gates |
| A machine rating point does not transfer to an installation | Thermal and field leads verify source, sink, auxiliaries and residual duty; revise the released operating domain and repeat the matched-service test | Requirements at M6 and each rating/acceptance campaign |
| Software or sensor faults create unsuitable control decisions | Embedded and supervisory leads retain independent limits, diagnostics, override and deterministic fallback; regression includes disturbances and recovery | Product software releases through M21 |
| A site or climatic window cannot supply the necessary evidence | Qin and the field lead secure the incremental access scope and use the planned independent climate/endurance sequence for the missing conditions | Site readiness review at M12, before M16 installation |
| Conformity or third-party rights require design changes | Quality lead and Frédéric obtain the applicable assessment, record corrective design or appropriate rights and close findings before the affected release | Design changes, independent tests and commercial release |
| Financing or grant payments arrive later than the cash plan | Frédéric and finance support phase hiring, procurement and deployment against available funds, reviewing delayed receipts before commitments | Monthly cash review and each major commitment |
| CETP and EIC claim the same work or asset use | Project-data resource and finance support reconcile deliverables, employee time, asset use and invoices before charging costs | Monthly allocation review and reporting |

Technical releases and commercial delivery remain connected without treating them as one date. The plan can deliver an accepted reference version of the existing platform while qualifying later CAU, control or manufacturing changes. Each subsequent customer receives the released configuration, supporting evidence and service commitments appropriate to that delivery. This preserves the business plan's early revenue mechanism and the EIC action's distinct industrialisation outcome.
