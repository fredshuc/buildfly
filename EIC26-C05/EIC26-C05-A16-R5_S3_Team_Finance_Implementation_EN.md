### Conformity and performance verification

WP3's quality/compliance lead and Qin will maintain the product-applicability matrix, risk assessment, technical file, applicable tests and pre-market conformity evidence. The route will address Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, generally applicable from 20 January 2027, and classify EMC, electrical-safety and pressure requirements against the released configuration. [S3-5] Water as the cooling-cycle working fluid reduces dependence on fluorinated refrigerants under Regulation (EU) 2024/573; auxiliary circuits receive their own applicability check. [S3-6] Measurement plans separate useful cooling, electricity, regeneration heat, external water and installation boundaries. Facility PUE/WUE reporting follows Regulation (EU) 2024/1364; internal regeneration heat is distinguished from externally reused energy. [S3-7] Testing will combine traceable calibration with laboratories whose competence and relevant scope are demonstrated against ISO/IEC 17025. [S3-8] Qualification uses an integrated-machine protocol, with relevant ASHRAE 143 and EN 13141-7 measurement methods applied within their scopes to the evaporative-cooling and air-handling subsystems. [S3-9, S3-10] Findings will be closed before the affected product release.

### Principal execution risks and responses

| Risk and delivery consequence | Response and accountable owner | Review point |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment delays affect the 213 PM planned-role allocation | Frédéric and Qin prioritise the M1 posts, match selection to first deliverables, assign deputies and rephase dependencies; scoped professional services support the tasks they actually cover | Before M1 commitments, then monthly and before each gate |
| CAU processing changes reduce output, consistency or life | Process and reliability leads use batch traceability, after-shaping tests, ageing/contamination checks and quarantined nonconforming lots | Batch releases and M12/M18 gates |
| A machine rating point does not transfer to an installation | Thermal and field leads verify source, sink, auxiliaries and residual duty; revise the released operating domain and repeat the matched-service test | Requirements at M6 and each rating/acceptance campaign |
| Software or sensor faults create unsuitable control decisions | Embedded and supervisory leads retain independent limits, diagnostics, override and deterministic fallback; regression includes disturbances and recovery | Product software releases through M21 |
| A site or climatic window cannot supply the necessary evidence | Qin and the field lead will secure incremental access, interfaces, operating responsibilities and windows before M12; independent climate/endurance tests supplement missing conditions alongside actual installation and field operation | Site readiness review at M12, before M16 installation |
| Conformity or third-party rights require design changes | Quality lead and Frédéric obtain the applicable assessment, record corrective design or appropriate rights and close findings before the affected release | Design changes, independent tests and commercial release |
| Financing or grant payments arrive later than the cash plan | Frédéric and finance support phase hiring, procurement and deployment against available funds, reviewing delayed receipts before commitments | Monthly cash review and each major commitment |
| CETP and EIC claim the same work or asset use | Project-data resource and finance support reconcile deliverables, employee time, asset use and invoices before charging costs | Monthly allocation review and reporting |
| Data-centre procurement, reference acceptance or replication orders arrive later than planned | Ina leads account qualification, reference-site operating evidence, staged offers and order/collection tracking | Quarterly pipeline review |
| Customers select existing compression/free-cooling equipment or a competing solution | Frédéric and Ina compare equal-service performance maps, installed cost and effective operating hours to demonstrate differentiation | Product release and key commercial offers |

Reference deliveries of the existing platform and qualification of subsequent CAU, control and manufacturing changes proceed through their respective release requirements. Each customer receives the released configuration, supporting evidence and service commitments appropriate to that delivery.
