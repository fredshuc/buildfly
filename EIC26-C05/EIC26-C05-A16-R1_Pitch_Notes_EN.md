# AirTransformer pitch speaker notes

Planned presentation time 10 minutes

## Slide 1 AirTransformer for urban critical facilities

Planned time 35 seconds

Savery Tech develops AirTransformer for urban facilities that must maintain cooling through heatwaves while managing power and water constraints. We have a complete TRL6 machine, Savery-owned system IP and a funded CETP collaboration with HPE providing the site path towards TRL7. This proposal advances a reproducible, reliable and supportable product to TRL8. We seek approximately €2.486 million grant and €2 million EIC investment to connect product qualification with manufacturing and deployment.

References: Savery current product, IP and CETP status supplied 3 November 2026; application budget.

## Slide 2 Cooling continuity and customer value

Planned time 45 seconds

Our customer's requirement is continued cooling under the site's actual utility and installation limits. The engineering objectives are sixty to seventy percent lower cooling electricity and forty to sixty percent lower external water withdrawal in suitable configurations. We will compare the complete installation at equal delivered service, including regeneration heat, heat rejection, auxiliaries and water treatment. A separate continuity assessment records compliant hours and recovery under utility caps. The owner approves the investment, the engineer specifies the interfaces and the operator accepts and maintains the equipment.

References: Product engineering objectives and comparison plan. Savings require matched configurations and service.

## Slide 3 System and site interfaces

Planned time 50 seconds

CAU contactors treat the air, with low-temperature heat restoring their working capacity. The cooling and regeneration paths connect to the required heat sink and electrical auxiliaries. Indirect evaporative cooling serves the assigned air-side duty, while any liquid-water recovery passes through collection, treatment and the intended-use assessment. We prioritise external make-up water savings at equal cooling service. Fifty to seventy degrees Celsius is the qualification heat-source range, with outlet temperature and humidity requirements specified at the delivery interface. All internal consumption, evaporation, storage and discarded water remain inside the installation balance.

References: Product technical plan and intended-use water qualification. Heat range is a qualification envelope.

## Slide 6 Competition and selection

Planned time 45 seconds

Customers can retain compression and economisation, improve water reuse or select another sorption or desiccant product. AirJoule already discloses low-grade waste heat use, so that feature alone does not establish differentiation. Its Prime water platform and Core DH dehumidification offering also have different performance boundaries from a cooling-duty comparison. Transaera, Mojave, atmospheric-water suppliers and Munters provide other purchasing alternatives. Savery's offer must win on accepted air-side cooling, net electricity, heat and water, installation cost and dependable support. Productisation makes this a testable equipment and service proposition.

References: AirJoule https://airjouletech.com/products/ ; Transaera and KCC manufacturer announcement, 29 September 2026; Mojave https://mojavehvac.com/products/arctidry-hydro/ ; Munters https://datacenter.munters.com/product/sycool-split/ .

## Slide 7 Initial serviceable market

Planned time 60 seconds

We define the initial 2031 annual serviceable market in the five urban colocation markets of Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris and Dublin. CBRE's third-quarter 2025 forecast totalled 624 megawatts of annual new capacity in those markets. We adopt a flat, rounded 600 megawatt planning reference rather than claiming a 2031 growth forecast. Ten percent assigned air-side cooling and twenty-five percent joint qualification give fifteen megawatts of serviceable duty. At twelve hundred euros per kilowatt installed, that is eighteen million euros annually. The assumptions are explicit, with a seven-point-two to twenty-eight-point-eight million euro qualification sensitivity. Our same-scope 2031 sales target is one-point-eight million euros, or ten percent.

References: CBRE European Data Centres Figures Q3 2025, full-year forecast for five primary markets. Savery 2031 planning assumptions: 600 MW flow, 10% duty, 25% joint qualification, EUR1200/kW installed.

## Slide 8 Market entry and economics

Planned time 60 seconds

Initial C05 sales follow product qualification: six hundred thousand euros in 2029, one-point-two million in 2030 and one-point-eight million in 2031. These correspond to half, one and one-and-a-half megawatts of accepted cooling in the defined scope. The hundred-kilowatt equivalent is a planning unit, not a released machine rating. A calculated hundred-kilowatt customer case, eight thousand annual hours and stated energy and water tariffs gives about twenty-five to twenty-eight months simple payback at a hundred-and-twenty-thousand-euro installed cost. Each offer uses the customer's actual load, heat cost and alternative installation. The wider company growth case remains separate from this market-share calculation.

References: Savery market-entry and customer-economic scenarios. EUR1100/kW hardware, EUR100/kW installation and engineering; independent configuration-specific quotation follows qualification.
