# buildfly

Engineering reviews and supporting tooling.

- [`dampers/c02-r11-dfm/`](dampers/c02-r11-dfm/DFM_REVIEW.md): DFM review and manufacturing-acceleration plan for the AT-DMP02 C02 R11 two-leaf air damper (red-team pack of 2026-10-12). It includes independent CAD checks (OCP), a re-implementation of the feedback-equaliser control model, closed-form screens, BOM process routing and responses to RT-01…RT-18.
- [`dampers/c02-r16-rt1/`](dampers/c02-r16-rt1/RT1_REVIEW.md): RT-1 independent review of the R16 full integration. It gives verified smallest fixes for the P stop and the front guard, the P orientation and wet-support decision, and kit release waves.
- [`dampers/c02-r21-rt2/`](dampers/c02-r21-rt2/RT2_REVIEW.md): RT-2 independent review of the R21 manufacturing master and the P/R cold-test configurations. It verifies the RT-1 adoptions, audits the BOM, maps the fasteners to standards, and proposes smallest changes for rotor support, film-end retention and stop/gear evidence, with an ordered acceleration plan.
- [`AirTransformerShell/revisions/C04/RT1_REVIEW/`](AirTransformerShell/revisions/C04/RT1_REVIEW/C04_RT1_REVIEW.md): RT-1 independent review of shell C04, the simplified two-bed, one-stage module with two adsorptive HX. It gives dispositions per module and for cold-flow readiness, CAD-verified interface blockers (damper spacing, bed-B valve mapping, seat and support design), an air-distribution risk screen and a single-bed cold-flow plan.
- [`AirTransformerShell/revisions/C06/RT2_REVIEW/`](AirTransformerShell/revisions/C06/RT2_REVIEW/C06_RT2_REVIEW.md): RT-2 independent review of shell C06 (D01 dampers at 1280 mm pitch, M101 port alignment, HYD-R1 water bay). It reproduces the build and integration, gives the closure status of each RT-1 finding, and lists five narrow geometry returns (seal carrier stiffness and reaction path, zero-clearance seal interfaces, captured drip tray, cheek slot ligaments) with smallest corrections.
- [`AirTransformerShell/revisions/C07/RT3_REVIEW/`](AirTransformerShell/revisions/C07/RT3_REVIEW/C07_RT3_REVIEW.md): RT3 independent review of the C07 two-bed shell and ducts at frozen commit 2272676. It reproduces the C07 pipeline and the internal RT3 evidence, gives the N1–N11 dispositions, and issues separate verdicts for prototype detailing (accept with conditions D1–D4), bed-A cold flow (accept after G0), and cutting, two-bed manufacture and live water (hold).
- [`AirTransformerShell/revisions/C07/RT3_REVIEW/TOPOLOGY_RESET/`](AirTransformerShell/revisions/C07/RT3_REVIEW/TOPOLOGY_RESET/CUBE_TOPOLOGY_RESET.md): topology reset after the owner rejected the C04–C07 air path. It is a cube module, about 1.64 × 1.62 × 1.52 m, with each vertical coil between two opposed sloped plenums: process air enters at the top and moves +X, regeneration air enters at the bottom and moves −X. The header model gives a face CV of about 1%; the path loss is about half that of C07. Concept input for shell2's next revision.
