# L2-HX response to C06 RT-2

5 November 2026. HX-only design candidate, based on review commit `205f9c2` and the preserved M101 C04 single-core STEP. No shell, water-bay or damper files are modified. Cutting, pressure manufacture, two-bed manufacture and live-water release remain on hold.

**N4 — returned HX geometry.** C05 carries the tray on the core, using four tabs on the two HX bottom crossbars. The two crossbars receive fixing-hole reservations; final blind M4 retention and tab joints still need detailing. The drain spigot retained on the core ends at installed Z1159, 9 mm above the Z1150 beam tops. Disconnect and remove the entire hose-side connector before sliding the core. Delete the sixteen superseded C06 tray, nozzle, pan-pad and tray-bar parts listed in `C05_CHECKS.json`.

Both outboard 900 mm service paths pass: A towards −Y, B towards +Y. For each of the 549 moving solids, its entire swept AABB avoids the retained shell solids after the specified service removals. This is a conservative continuous geometric exclusion for those shell obstacles. It is conditional on released seal carriers, removed water spools and detached drain hose; it does not certify tolerances, actual purchased connectors, rigging, cables or a physical service trial. C06's existing side-panel/actuator removal requirements still apply.

**N7 — one current HX function record, with qualification holds.** The C06/HYD-R1 prototype table adopts W1 = CS/HS and W2 = CR/HR on both beds. B uses the C06 translation `(265,477.825,1150)`, not the old 840 mm-pitch placement. This is the successor L2-HX conditional design signoff for prototype integration; hydronics must reconcile its installation and control records against the table. Historical hot operation W2→W1 remains the stopped-and-replumbed comparison configuration. Regeneration equivalence has not been established.

C05 proposes one independent upward vent branch on each terminal high-level water run at local X235. Vent boss faces are Z1252, closure envelopes reach Z1257, and the top tool budgets reach Z1297 (installed Z2447). A topology check connects both ideal ID4 vent branches to the preserved nominal water domain as a single valid solid. This does not demonstrate purge performance. Actual pressure-rated vents, joining details, source pressure/relief arrangements, two-direction filling/purging and the thermal comparison remain prerequisites. Live water stays held pending the required integration and tests.

**N1–N3 and N9 — mating inputs, not whole-machine closure.** The HX retains its 20 mm frame lands. Eight OD8 tie-rod references and OD12 allowance corridors are supplied at the red team's proposed lateral locations, with exact detailed-HX clearance results in the checks. Two OD12 removable top-pin references fit the existing 13.2×21.2 vertical lug slots. Their 0.6 mm lateral clearance is not a qualified vibration restraint. Shell owner to complete the stiff carrier, local force loop, tip setback, secondary seal, connected top structure, X–Z bracing and load/modal evidence.

The full STEP reads back as 549 valid solids: 533 original C04 parts retained, four replaced and twelve added. The circuit tubes, 39-branch headers, 306 fins, shoes, seal lands, upper lugs and OEM union faces are preserved. New/changed product parts, installed HX/shell pairs and vent-tool/shell pairs pass the 0.001 mm³ collision criterion. Four current port coordinates and functions are checked against C06's hydronics register. Full numbers, input identities and hashes accompany the source.

N5/N8/N10/N11, selector-box G0 and R-port vane provisions remain with shell/integration. N6 still requires a hose route through a defined floor penetration to an external trap; the new HX spigot is an interface, not a completed drainage system. The C04 C1–C8 conditions and RT2 N1–N6 cold-flow gates remain applicable.
