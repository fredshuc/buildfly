# Independent review of the first C09 integration export

This review applies to the first S1 and S2 exports. S1 SHA-256 is ff92ce01242f80e492471a6d609761f68fdbd72032c00fb89cfaae2e8e1f18a0. S2 SHA-256 is 108d1b512858b90e40510560bc4929c901c9386974ea117196bce64a8982beab. Use the current integration handoff and regression evidence to determine which findings later exports close.

**Disposition at these two hashes. Return the host for geometry correction. Hold cutting, wet operation and air performance release.**

The reimport register contains 477 host, 230 damper and 2,196 HX occurrences per state. It contains no water pipes or valves. The two complete air-side states are a useful engineering comparison. The original 521-occurrence sheet assembly contributes 477 host items after D03 takes ownership of 44 original names.

| B outer skin has a false thick patch | The original B skin bounds span Y838.5–850 mm because they include a return. The patch uses that complete 11.5 mm depth. The A skin spans only 1.5 mm. The new B skin intersects its rail by 23,760 mm³ and the lower damper backer by 1,785 mm³. Both pairs were clear in the parent. | Rebuild the aperture in the local flat thickness and repeat the shell comparison. |
| B feedback covers cross the outer air sheet | Both S1 and S2 give 464 mm³ at the lower cover and 232 mm³ at the upper cover. The corresponding removal envelopes intersect by 9,284 and 4,642 mm³. The 46 movement envelopes have zero positive-volume intersections with the host. | Provide sealed interfaces at both locations and check installed and removal geometry independently. |
| Regen divider loses edge contact on both sides of both beds | I cut vertical BRep lines on a 1 mm X grid near each cheek and divider interface. From X721 through 729 mm, the divider lower face lies above the cheek. Peak measured gap 0.303395 mm at X727. Approximate projected open area 1.42 mm² per edge. Parent A-low at X727 had about 2.07 mm of vertical overlap. | Map cheeks and divider through the same piecewise X transformation, or detail a sealing flange. Check continuous edge coverage. The aperture is a geometric measure. Actual leakage requires differential pressure and a leak test. |
| Stretched regen floor changes gauge | The old 1.5 mm lower floor has slope about 0.64134. The X531–727 segment maps to 86 mm rather than 196 mm. Normal thickness implied by this transform is about 1.006 mm there. Vertical edge intercepts of about 1.785 mm on the remapped plane are consistent with this value. | Generate constant-thickness sheet bodies from the new neutral surfaces before flattening or ordering sheet. |
| Detailed HX intersects inherited host features | First integration screen reports 90 HX–shell pairs over 0.001 mm³. The eight original side masks each sum to about 51,611 mm³ of intersection with HX leaves. The A rear post sums to 23,796 mm³. Both upper splitters and both rear air sheets also appear. | Resolve physical interpenetrations on a named owner basis. Trimming around a coil must preserve a defined seating land, air seal and extraction path. A nominal tangent cut provides no gasket or tolerance allowance. |
| Hydronics and operating limits require a separate decision | Actual HX trial is the four-instance non-handed C05 with downward H00 terminals. The owner route for hot operation traverses the bed in the opposite order to cold operation. The earlier generic per-core W1 supply/W2 return register cannot serve as the installed hot/cold register without reconciling reversal. Nominal P0, if unavailable as geometry, only provides a routing basis. | Recover the owner's versioned water CAD and verified port directions. Check the short spools, wrench and service envelopes, isolation, drains, vents and clearance to both D03 states. H00 alone cannot freeze shell penetrations or qualify a wet build. |

**Independent sampling.** I reread 13 A-bed lower air-interface components from the original and first C09 files and compared all 78 pairwise nearest distances. Among pairs less than 5 mm apart in the parent, no global minimum grew by more than 0.05 mm. The four local edge gaps above demonstrate why a nearest-point or clash-only result cannot establish a seal. The leakage fraction, velocity distribution, strength and tolerance stack were not measured. This review is a geometric screening result.
