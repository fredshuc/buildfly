## Assembly order and datum control

1. Fixture the main frame and independent water skid on the Z26 floor datum. Establish level and six-foot contact; record substrate, anchor product and tightening procedure. Do not use the skin or HX nozzles to level the water island.
2. Prepare and qualify the eight 3 mm A-post CJP joints. Weld in opposing short backsteps, cool, inspect root fusion and outer flush faces, then survey the frame and frozen HX support coordinates. Fit seals only after welding, grinding, cleaning and coating.
3. Fit the four face carriers, three bearing pads per core, positive locators and hard stops. Record the loaded core bottom coordinates Z620/955 and all eight water-port coordinates. Do not change core elevation to accommodate a thicker seal. Use temporary rated supports until all clamps are secured; the three-pad triangle and actual CG must be checked before removing them.
4. Install the selected face strips on support liner, make the qualified scarf joint, measure free thickness and land flatness, then tighten opposed clamps in stages to the approved load. Nominal 0.5 Nm is a candidate setting; the HX owner must first approve the frame force, local contact loads and thermal sliding mechanism.
5. Fit water-rack feet and rigid/sliding pipe supports to the approved reaction schedule. Align spools without pulling on HX nozzles. Record free-fit offsets and nozzle residual loads; verify both TA returns, FS/Q branches and eight two-way selector valves against the owner state matrix.
6. Resolve the pocket/Pin/crown/upper-vent joint before fitting its replaceable gasket. Inspect the Shell-owned front wall, land, two hard stops and seat access. The front wall may not be omitted to obtain clearance.
7. Fit and qualify skin glands, H02 service penetrations, condensate supports, flexible joints and separate atmospheric receptors. The service-hole and fixing-hole cuts do not constitute completed air/vapour seals. The four air-side traps never receive pressure-water relief discharge.
8. Install the blank distribution frames, rail locks and test pickups. Verify all dampers through the full stroke, close the roof with renewed perimeter seal and bolt caps, then perform the following dry tests before any water operation.
