This register accompanies the Shell review candidate. It is not an approval or a request to fabricate. The final interference register and its matching STEP hashes control geometric findings; development results are retained separately.

Eight A-post seams now have preparation geometry, effective throat/length and conditional transfer/capacity calculations. Release still requires the specified thin-wall CJP procedure, root-fusion evidence, material certificates, actual joint/foundation assumptions and loaded datum survey. Physical tests in the test plan remain unperformed.

The sum of face-clamp bolt preloads is not a demonstrated seal or toe reaction. Contact stiffness, sleeve/stop seating and jaw fit determine that split. Accept measured contact force and the complete cold/hot seal gap, rather than inferring them from torque alone.

The tested gland guard route is not accepted. Lower guards encounter the local H02 drain adapters and mounting bolts during radial removal; upper guards have unresolved contact with their flexible lips. Qualify a real local drain-hardware predecessor sequence or change guard segmentation, with isolation, tools and opposite-level support included. No hidden deletion of those pressure parts is used to obtain a service pass.
