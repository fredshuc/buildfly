# Rejected zero-clearance jaw edge

The 40 mm station-17 jaw had no positive-volume intersection, but its right edge at X=-390 touched the H02 condensate support rail (nominal distance about 1.7e-13 mm). The fluid/collector distance check identified this during final validation. The final jaw is trimmed 1 mm on its right edge to X=-391, maintaining the M5 axis X=-410 and all core datums. Its net section and drawing are recalculated. Only the final current-hash clearance and assembly evidence controls acceptance. These files preserve the failed 40 mm iteration.
