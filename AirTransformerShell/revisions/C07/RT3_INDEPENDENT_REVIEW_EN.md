# Independent integration review of AirTransformer C07

**Decision.** Accept C07 as the two-bed geometry reference for prototype detailing and for the regeneration-first selector-box study. Keep production cutting and two-bed manufacture on hold. Build the first full-size cold-flow article as bed A on its independently braced stand after RT1 G0 and the stated build gates. Live-water operation requires separate hydronics and HX qualification.

**Scope.** An independent review agent inspected the C07 shell STEP, named C05 HX geometry, D02 support and R2 water evidence, the shell builder, and the integration and service checks. The contact measurements below were rerun from STEP for A-front, A-rear, B-front and B-rear. This is an internal follow-up to C06 RT2. The original external review team has yet to review C07.

| RT2 finding | C07 evidence and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| N1–N2, face clamp | Each of the four gaskets has 37,632 mm² of face area. Subtracting the **union** of the detailed HX lands leaves 0 mm² uncovered. All 14 stops land fully, 48 mm² each. The 14 cross-HX tie rods per bed close the force loop between the two carrier frames and the HX. The 60 × 20 × 3 mm rectangular-section rail has nominal weak-axis second moment 27,652 mm⁴. At 60 kPa assumed gasket pressure, a 12 mm line width and a deliberately long 400 mm simply supported span give 0.043 mm straight-rail deflection. The 1.8 mm stroke must still be verified by pressure film. Hard-stop machining, corner joints, frame flexibility and the actual gasket load curve set the residual preload. |
| N3, secondary seal | The revised moving and fixed elastomer flanges have full metal support on all four faces, measuring 21,489 and 23,100 mm² per face respectively. The earlier fixed end provided only a 0.20 mm bond across the top and bottom edges. The revised fixed flange is 7 mm wide and the moving flange 6.5 mm. Each fixed collar touches both cheeks and the outer guide sheets. Specify continuous sealed joints. The retracting CAD profile preserves the nominal 23.15 mm side and 20.61 mm top/bottom meridional lengths across the 6 mm stroke. The closest nominal vertical collar-to-carrier rail clearance is 1 mm per side. Cycle a sample built at fit-up limits under both pressure signs. Inspect the corners and measure leakage. |
| N4, N10, core service | The tray and short spigot travel with each core. The pocketed service nuts clear the clamped rail by 2.05 mm. Four guides per carrier have a geometric shoulder at a 6 mm park stroke. STEP shows 78.43 mm² push-nut and 133.13 mm² shoulder bearing per guide in the parked state. A nut advances 8.05 mm to capture the parked rail. The guide brackets each land on 96 mm² of cheek. Unwind the primary ties progressively, hold each carrier square with its service guides and confirm positive contact at the four park stops before moving the core. The integrator's continuous 900 mm A/B paths include the captive tray. Disconnect the hose-side fitting and two X615–805 detachable spools. Retain the vented water trees from X805. Trial with the selected connector, rigging and level transfer rails. |
| N5, N11, selector | Adjacent 113 mm service slots at 125 mm pitch leave 12 mm of intact web. Individual 800 mm screen withdrawals, 280 mm forward rod parking and split upper-skin openings are defined. The added HX recess probes and regeneration vane fixing points support the proposed test. Nominal passage coverage does not establish the flow distribution. |
| N6, drains | Six individual traps have 90 mm conservative clear water head (about 0.88 kPa). Verify priming, pull-through, dry cap and drainage at the actual fan suction. The C05 tray falls 8 mm in 612 mm. Its Ø18 outlet is centred 68 mm before the low edge. Ideal level installation leaves a roughly 0.75 mm deep water wedge there. Run a pour test and have the HX owner dimple or regrade the floor if the no-pooling requirement is imposed. The separate water-bay tail requires a removable bore seal and an external receptor. |
| N7–N8, water and dampers | HX and water registers agree on W1 supply and W2 return for both circuits at the four +X unions. Purge/relief, valve sequence and hot-direction thermal comparison remain open. D02 carries both damper flanges through independent inner/outer bearing locations and the common skid. Measure actual contact under selected damper weights. Bed-B right-hand qualification, G843 and actuator selection remain open. |
| N9, stand | Each riser diagonal has 1,120 mm² of contact at both ends. Each end-plane diagonal lands on both posts, 480 mm² at each end. The permanent service brace meets the top cross over 576 mm² and the foot over 608 mm². Its 34 × 20 mm foot sits above a base cross-member through the 2 mm floor. Detail the shear connection, welds and anchors, and verify racking, hold-down and modal response with the service panel removed. |

**Water service sequence.** After isolation, drain-down and release of both unions, unfasten the shared gland and move it 60 mm in +X, from X750–752 to X810–812. Its two Ø110 openings pass over both Ø70 collars. The parked plate leaves 5 mm between the spool ends and the gland. Secure it there. Remove A.W2 then A.W1 in −Y for bed A, or B.W1 then B.W2 in +Y for bed B. For each assembly, a filled swept envelope of the Ø33.7 spool and both Ø70 collars cleared 930 mm of lateral travel with the X805-onward vented trees retained. The retained solid check used 494 shell, 1,098 HX, 44 water and 149 D02 occurrences. The 7,212 D01 occurrence bounds supplied no candidate overlap. The CAD assumes separable union faces. Specify accessible gland fasteners and a method to support the parked plate. Verify thread, nose and insulation clearances with the selected unions.

The four HX rails/posts overlap at each source CAD corner by 20 × 20 × 8 mm. This inherited solid representation explains the duplicated area when four land-contact values are added. Specify the finished joint and flatness in the HX fabrication definition.

For **G0**, use equal flow and the actual selector geometry, with the smaller regeneration inlet first in the intended direction. Evaluate the full-size box by CFD or mock-up before cutting. For the dry G1 article, record 8 × 12 face patches per mode, each 0.80–1.20 of the mean, coefficient of variation ≤10%, seat bypass ≤0.2% per bed and tracer cross-leak per RT1. Retain C1–C8, including the fan and test airway. D01 supplies the break-before-make air state table. Update its T3 water permissive for eight two-way valves and prove feedback, zero-flow thresholds and selected actuator times before running. G2 and G3 cover plate tuning and wet operation. Neither CFD nor structural FE nor a physical test was performed in this review.

## Reproduction

The audited shell SHA-256 is `c0fcd1f3bc39a033dfaf223668b8cc3754635c2fc7ad4e59858ba959b9ec6f94`. Run `python rt3_contact_check.py` beside the final STEP after unpacking C04 and M101 C05 as described in the shell handoff. The measured land unions, 56 stop contacts, all 16 parking positions, diaphragm flange areas and frame contacts are in `rt3_contacts.json`. With all owner packages extracted, run `python rt3_water_access.py` for the gland and spool sweeps in `rt3_water_access.json`. Project integration, motion, plate-access and HX-extraction results are separately attributed to the C07 integration team.
