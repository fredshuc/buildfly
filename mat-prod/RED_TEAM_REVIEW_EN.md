## Questions for reviewers

1. **Excellence and novelty.** Is the breakthrough sufficiently specific against the materials, shaped-adsorber and mechanically assisted heat-transformer literature? Check whether the design separates material, shaping, transport, pressure assistance and control effects. Inspect the matched-service, full-reset and independent-block evidence and the incremental claim for constrained RL against tuned MPC.
2. **Impact and topic fit.** Does the proposal connect the physical materials and manufacturing results to specific IAM4EU objectives, an industrial uptake path and credible TRL 4 outputs? Check that the energy figures are scenario context and that the project-level claims match the evidence.
3. **Implementation and integration.** Are the activities, facilities, staff, budget and schedule sufficient for the work and independent validation proposed? Trace each material and control claim to a task, a deliverable and an acceptance condition. Check production ownership, manufacturing release, metering and recovery capacity.
4. **Cross-section consistency.** Check the five-party consortium, 36 months, Savery’s single nominal 5 kg synthesis line and the single nominal 10 kW laboratory module. The 45 °C source and 70 °C delivery are an application example. The laboratory design point uses a 55 °C source, 25 °C rejection and 65→70 °C useful water. The 144 prospective histories are planned and are nested within three independent build blocks.
5. **Editorial and evidence gaps.** Flag any unsupported performance, novelty or readiness claim. S1 is the scientific longform and will need a separate page-limit pass. The S3 T7.2 production-inventory sentence is a known carryover. The proposed correction assigns synthesis and production records to Savery, forming records to IKTS, and independent materials characterisation and ageing evidence to IRCELYON.

Please log one finding per item, with **severity**, **section and locator**, **evidence**, **consequence** and **proposed change**. Use **Blocker** for an issue that prevents submission or invalidates a central claim, **Major** for an issue requiring substantive correction and **Minor** for a local clarification. End with an overall review disposition and the three changes that would most improve the submission.
